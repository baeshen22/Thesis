## 5. What the visualisations are and are not

- They are path-traced (Blender Cycles) renders of a 3D model built **directly from the DWG footprints**. Every view shows the same model, so geometry is consistent across images.
- They are **concept visualisations**. Vehicles, people and palms are simplified procedural models, and context massing is generic. They are not photographic marketing renders and not technical drawings.
- No AI image generation was used. No real brand marks appear in the new renders. Brand marks appear only in reproduced source pages, which are labelled as source material.
- Separation: no AlBalad Development Company (BDC) material or assumptions were used.
